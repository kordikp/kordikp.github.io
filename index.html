Pavel Kordík

Associate professor at FIT CTU in Prague · Co-founder of Recombee

I work on machine learning at FIT CTU in Prague and co-founded Recombee, where much of the research ends up in production. I try to use AI to make things around us a little better: recommenders that help people find what they would otherwise miss, AI that is safe for children at school, climate models for cities and forests, and AI agents that work for people.

500+ customers in 40+ countries and 10,000+ sites and apps, from media such as The Telegraph and DAZN to e-commerce and marketplaces. Annual recurring revenue of about USD 8M.

Research

What I work on with PhD students and colleagues at FIT CTU and with the research team at Recombee: recommendation and search, trustworthy AI agents, machine learning for weather and climate, and AI for science and learning.

Personalisation & search · Recombee Lab

Language models that learn taste

beeFormer puts a language model inside the training loop of a recommender, and recall on books it has never seen more than doubles (RecSys 2024). CompresSAE makes embeddings 12× smaller and runs in production (RecSys 2025); Sparse ELSA makes them readable (The Web Conference 2026); at The Telegraph, editors use the same idea to see which reader groups engage with which topics.

Weather & climate · FOCAL

From tomorrow's rain to the end of the century

WeatherFusionNet predicts rain eight hours ahead from satellite images and won Weather4cast at NeurIPS 2022. In the Horizon Europe project FOCAL we bring climate projections from 12.5 km down to under 500 m, with error bars, for city heat and forest growth. This line of work led group members to found Tempest Labs in 2025.

Trustworthy AI agents · PoliRuralPlus

Agents that know the place, the child and the market

The JackDaw GeoAI agent and AgriBot answer farmers' questions with local data. Our guards catch advice that breaks a local rule (44 → 86 % of harmful replies, GeoAI 2026) and harms aimed at children (38 → 99 % recall on our benchmark, WOAH 2026). And when both the buyer and the seller are agents, we measure who should do the work (IJCCI 2026).

AI for science & learning

AI that does research, verifiably

In AutoResearch, agents run the experiment loop and a hidden test set confirms every result. Recommender maths chooses fluid-dynamics models with HZDR Dresden, and machine learning tunes laser–plasma experiments with ELI Beamlines and UC Berkeley. ResearchCoach, our AI mentor, comments on students' research drafts within minutes.

EU Research Projects

European projects where I am the Principal Investigator for CTU.

Horizon Europe · 2024–2026 · CTU coordinates

PoliRuralPlus

Place-based development of rural and peri-urban regions, with 20 partners from 13 countries, 9 pilot regions and 26 open-call teams. Our group builds the AI layer: the JackDaw GeoAI agent, AgriBot, which tells farmers which subsidies apply to their parcels, and safety guards that know the local rules.

poliruralplus.eu Agents in the Field (talk) Policy p-book
Horizon Europe · 2024–2027 · coordinated by Fraunhofer IGD

FOCAL

Local climate information for adaptation planning. Our group leads the AI models: super-resolution networks and Bayesian neural fields bring climate projections from 12.5 km to under 500 m and say how sure they are. They feed two pilots, urban heat in Constance and tree growth in Czech forests.

focal-euproject.eu

capTTict Completed

Interreg Danube, January 2024 – June 2026. Technology transfer, company building and ICT innovation across the Danube region: CTU was the lead partner of 16 partners from 9 countries, working with digital innovation hubs and experts.ai. In January 2026 we hosted the ICT Technology Transfer Days at FIT CTU: more than 25 incubated startups from across the Danube region pitched to investors in three tracks. Project page

Teaching

Academic Courses at CTU

Data Mining Algorithms (NI-ADM)

Advanced data mining with a focus on modern recommender systems and search, and the theory behind them, such as the bias–variance decomposition and modelling a model's confidence.

Computational Intelligence Methods (NI-MVI)

Modern AI architectures and optimisation methods, from the theory to working implementations. The course has its own living p-book.

Discussions of current AI research and breakthrough papers, where students practise reading critically and presenting research.
